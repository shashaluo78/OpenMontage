Measured: integrated loudness -13.8 LUFS (loud, social-ready); a light low-end beat around 112 BPM sits under the whole ad; the only speech-free moment is the first demo glide, where the steam hiss carries the sound.

Why it sells: a 1-second urgency hook, a pain line, an objection answered ("Is it gonna hurt?" / "Not with this one"), an instant payoff reaction, a habit line ("every meet, every time"), then fast feature + range proof over the demos.

## Recommended audio stack

1. **Voiceover (mom's voice, warm, slightly breathless, UGC delivery).** Our characters don't speak on camera, so a voiceover avoids lip-sync problems. Original wording, same selling beats as the reference:
   - 0.3-4.8 (Clip 1): "Medal photos in five minutes... and this comb is losing."
   - 5.6-10.4 (Clip 3 strokes): "Then he grabs the steam brush. One... two... three." (each number lands on a stroke)
   - 10.8-12.3 (mom's hands over her mouth): "No way."
   - 12.7-17.4 (Demo 5): "Warm steam, one smooth glide, and the curls just give up."
   - 17.9-22.7 (Demo 6): "Curly, frizzy, wavy. It lives in our gym bag now. Every meet."
2. **Music bed:** playful, upbeat light pop around 110-115 BPM (claps, finger snaps, plucky bass). Sparse and slightly tense under Clip 1, then it lifts on the first brush stroke at 5.2s. Ducked about 12 dB under the voice.
3. **Sound effects:** a comb snag at about 1-2s; a soft steam "psssh" on each of dad's three strokes and on each demo glide (this is the product's signature sound); native crowd ambience kept low under Clips 1 and 3.
4. **Mix:** final loudness about -14 LUFS to match the reference and platform norms; short fades at every cut.
